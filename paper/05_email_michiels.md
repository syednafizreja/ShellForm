**Subject:** Question on the inputs of your seismic form-finding examples (C&S 2018, J. IASS 2017)

Dear Dr Michiels,

I am a structural engineer working on the embodied carbon of reinforced concrete shells. My study extends your work with Professor Adriaenssens on form-finding for lateral load ("Form-finding algorithm for masonry arches subjected to in-plane earthquake loading", Computers & Structures 195, 2018; and the 2017 J. IASS paper on masonry shells). I compare gravity-only and seismic-aware forms of 10 m and 20 m shells sized to code checks. The code and pre-registration are open: https://github.com/syednafizreja/ShellForm.

I would like to reproduce one of your published arch cases as a check of my solver. I do not have access to the full text of the C&S paper or the Engineering Structures papers. Could you share an author copy, or the stated inputs (geometry, thickness, load and acceptance tolerance) of one arch case?

One early result may interest you: in reinforced concrete shells of this size, the carbon is set by the buckling check and the support condition rather than by the seismic level, so the seismic-aware form gives no meaningful carbon difference. I expect this to change in your masonry setting, and I would value your view on where the boundary lies.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
