## 1. Plan-area fix (pre-registration amendment of 3 Nov)

Carbon and steel per m² are now divided by the plan area the shell covers (`shellform.fe.plan_area`), not span². The T1 scaling uses the same plan area for the SDL and live-load mass. Corner shells cover 73.34 m² at 10 m. The 10 m results were rerun with the fix:

- No thickness, governing check, buckling factor or steel mass changes.
- Corner T1 falls by 5.3 to 6.2 %. T1 enters only S5 (T1 < Tg = 0.35 s): the longest T1 is now 0.184 s at 10 m (was 0.194 s) and 0.308 s at 20 m, so S5 holds at both spans.
- Corner carbon rises from 24.9-33.4 to 34.0-45.5 kgCO₂e/m²; edges stay at 21.6-23.8.
- Strategy verdicts, relative differences and costs of resilience are identical.

## 2. 20 m sizing

- All 84 designs are feasible.
- Edge-supported shells: 50 mm everywhere, governed by the minimum thickness (as at 10 m).
- Corner-supported shells: 265 mm (rise 0.10), 215 mm (0.15), 185 mm (0.20), governed by buckling. At 10 m they were 75 to 105 mm.
- One difference between the strategies: at rise 0.10, Sa 0.5 and 0.6 g, the seismic form takes 260 mm against 265 mm for the gravity form. Its λ is 5.003 and 5.012, just above the 5.0 limit, so this is the same grid-step effect as the 85 against 90 mm rows at 10 m (D4-5). At 10 m a 61 x 61 mesh moved such shells below 5.0. It is not evidence of a strategy effect [J].

## 3. Steel, support zones and carbon at 20 m

- Minimum steel governs in 38 of 84 designs. At 10 m it governed in 42 of 84, and the others were at most 10.5 kg above the minimum. Above the minimum, corner shells need at most 283.5 kg more, against 2953 kg in total; edges at most 1.0 kg.
- The seismic case adds at most 57.9 kg of steel to any corner shell (0.82 kg at 10 m) and 0.95 kg to any edge shell. So steel does rise above the minimum at 20 m, as the Day 5 lesson expected, but mostly under gravity.
- Support-zone thickening adds at most 1.8 % concrete (0.6 % at 10 m).
- Carbon: corners 84.7-117.3 kgCO₂e/m², edges 21.6-23.8 kgCO₂e/m² of plan. Concrete gives 80 to 81 %.

## 4. Decision rules (pre-registration section 7)

- "No meaningful difference" in all 42 cells, at the central factors and at all four band corners.
- Cost of resilience: gravity strategy 1.000-1.004, seismic strategy 0.983-1.015.
- Headline claim not made.
